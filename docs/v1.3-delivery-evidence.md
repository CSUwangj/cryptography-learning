## Authority and proof boundaries

The parent criteria A1-A19 and its linked R1-R7 decisions are authoritative.
[P13-SUMMARY-2](https://github.com/CSUWangj/cryptography-learning/issues/140#issuecomment-6752140614)
partially supersedes the older summary contract. The five visible columns are
Stage, Baseline result, Changed result, Changed bits and Ratio, with Chinese
阶段, 基准结果, 改变后结果, 改变位数 and 比例. Runtime masks remain retained;
there is no summary mask column. Pairing, arithmetic, gaps, grouping and lineage
retain their approved semantics.

The existing compiler/session/Worker-handler tests prove execution, validation
and immutable results with in-process transport. RenderHost/shared-component
tests prove the resulting presentation and state. Real-Lesson Chromium tests
prove native Worker/browser interaction and bounded layout. Mocking document
delivery in some browser cases does not mock the Worker, but it also does not
verify a live backend. The actual-demo cases additionally verify catalog entries
and the documented routes. Source and test links below identify coverage; the
run and reuse ledger states which evidence actually ran and where it applies.

## Historical evidence and remaining limits

The accepted November 5 comparison prototype used fixed 32-bit excerpts. Its
typecheck, 140 selected tests, 16 Chromium regressions and focused inspection do
not prove complete paired v2 Worker execution, full AES traces, decryption,
standalone initialization or missing-checkpoint integration. The classical
prototype remains design evidence with its original drag, keyboard and animation
verification limits. Current real-Lesson checks, rather than prototype acceptance,
support the integration map. The [v1.2 map](v1.2-delivery-evidence.md) and
[November 2 audit](lesson-version-audit.md) retain their own dates and limits.

#143's original full AES-128 browser fixture failed its default visibility
assertion. The cause remains unknown. Its passing interactive fixture retains
one real generation; full AES-128/192/256 schedule correctness is runtime-boundary
evidence. A later full AES-128 #144 demo does not retroactively repair or prove
that earlier standalone visibility case.

#144 preserves dense complete paired overlays and local horizontal scrolling.
The detailed geometry test uses a bounded first-generation AES-192 fixture;
the actual-demo test retains complete AES-128 traces in both directions. Neither
is a full-schedule latency guarantee or a browser proof for every AES variant.
Earlier cold-visibility/assertion corrections, interrupted attempts and obsolete
summary-mask assertions remain in their original evidence history.

#145's first frozen unit attempt passed 290 of 291 tests and timed out in an
unchanged key-overlay test at 5000 ms. The identical-command recheck passed all
291 tests. That does not identify a cause or justify calling the test flaky.

#146's original demo/locale failures and two subsequent ciphertext-producer/
readonly repair rounds remain preserved. The final repair tests cover earlier
Vigenere/affine metadata and readonly earlier-bound Caesar through the actual
Worker handler in-process. They do not prove browser scheduling or geometry for
those new cross-Step cases. Earlier layout/theme evidence applies only to its
unchanged inputs; it is not proof of the ownership repair.

Historical attempts and consumed investigation budgets remain attached to their
failure rules. A new role, ticket or snapshot does not reset them. A required
failed, unavailable or unrun check remains incomplete unless an explicit
acceptance exception is authorized. No exception is recorded here.

The November 4 Markdown assessment records the locked `react-markdown` 4.3.1 /
`remark-parse` 5.0.0 chain's deprecated `trim` 0.0.1 and denial-of-service
advisory, the old React peer contract and archived container plugin. Those
dependency and maintenance limitations remain accepted. Students do not author
Markdown for these renderers under the approved input boundary. Retention is not
a claim that the chain is repaired or that arbitrary documents cannot stall the
browser. The separate rendering exploration remains outside this release.

No private Lesson migration, deployment, live Host inspection, benchmark,
non-Chromium certification or nightly whole-repository run is supplied by this
handoff. Recovery remains correcting authored documents/inputs and reloading or
rerunning the existing Lesson flow. Final staged checks and independent review
remain the coordinator's responsibility.
